# Feeding Guidelines

## Meal Timing
Feed pets at the same times each day; a predictable schedule aids digestion and behavior. Morning feeding around 07:00 and evening around 18:00 works for most households.

## Portion Control
Measure food rather than guessing, following the package guide adjusted for your pet's weight and activity. Treats should stay under 10 percent of daily calories.

## Hydration
Refresh water bowls at least once a day and wash them weekly. Pets generally drink more after exercise and in warm weather.
