# Cat Care

## Litter Box Care
Scoop the litter box daily and fully change the litter about once a week. Provide one box per cat plus one extra, placed in quiet, low-traffic spots.

## Feeding Routine
Adult cats typically eat two measured meals a day, morning and evening. Avoid free-feeding dry food all day for cats prone to overeating, and refresh water bowls daily.

## Play and Enrichment
Give cats two play sessions of 10 to 15 minutes a day using wand toys or chase games that mimic hunting. Vertical spaces like cat trees and window perches reduce stress.

## Grooming
Brush short-haired cats weekly and long-haired cats daily to prevent mats and hairballs. Most cats groom themselves and rarely need baths.
