# Dog Care

## Exercise and Walks
Adult dogs generally need two walks a day of about 30 minutes each, once in the morning and once in the evening. High-energy breeds may need longer outings or added play. Regular walks support weight, joints, and behavior.

## Feeding Routine
Most adult dogs do well with two measured meals a day, roughly 12 hours apart. Feed at consistent times, such as 07:00 and 18:00, and keep fresh water available at all times.

## Dental Care
Brush a dog's teeth several times a week, ideally daily, using dog-safe toothpaste. Dental chews can supplement brushing but do not replace it.

## Training and Enrichment
Short training sessions of 10 to 15 minutes a day keep dogs mentally engaged. Rotate toys and use puzzle feeders to prevent boredom and destructive behavior.
