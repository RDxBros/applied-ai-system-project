# Life Stage Care

## Puppies and Kittens
Young pets under one year need more frequent meals, usually three to four small meals a day. Keep play and training sessions short, 5 to 10 minutes, and prioritize socialization during the first months.

## Adult Pets
Adult pets between one and eight years old thrive on a consistent daily routine of feeding, exercise, and rest. Two meals and regular activity each day keep most adults healthy.

## Senior Pets
Senior pets aged eight years and older benefit from shorter, gentler exercise sessions and more frequent health check-ins. Watch for stiffness or appetite changes and keep walks around 15 to 20 minutes at an easy pace.
