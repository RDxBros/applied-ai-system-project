# Medication Guidelines

## Consistent Timing
Give medications at the same time each day to keep levels steady, such as 08:00 every morning. Pairing doses with a daily routine like breakfast reduces missed doses.

## Missed Doses
If a dose is missed, give it when remembered unless the next dose is close, then skip it. Never double a dose without professional guidance.

## Preventatives
Flea, tick, and heartworm preventatives are typically given once a month on a set date. Mark a recurring monthly reminder so doses are not skipped.
