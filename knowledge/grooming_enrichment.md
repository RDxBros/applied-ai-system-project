# Grooming and Enrichment

## Brushing and Bathing
Brush most pets at least weekly to reduce shedding and distribute skin oils. Bathe dogs about once a month; over-bathing dries out skin.

## Nail and Ear Care
Trim nails every three to four weeks, or when you hear clicking on hard floors. Check ears weekly for redness or odor and clean gently as needed.

## Daily Enrichment
Provide 15 to 30 minutes of enrichment daily through puzzle toys, scent games, or training. Rotating activities keeps pets engaged and reduces anxiety behaviors.
